## XIII Death
Something is to be changed. This is part of the journey. 

The ideals you held, visions you dreamt, deep feelings you attached.  It's time to revisit and take a different approach or it's time to grow out of it as it's not helping you.

It's not easy but those deep feelings need to be released.
