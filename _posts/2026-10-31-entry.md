## IV Four of Cups

You are sitting in the middle of what you have built, bored by its warmth and certain that nothing offered here will satisfy you.

However, stand and accept the ordinary gift in front of you, letting it anchor your restlessness rather than sinking deeper into the belief that you are owed more.
