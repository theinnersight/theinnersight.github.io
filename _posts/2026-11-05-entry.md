## VI Six of Pentacles

You are caught in a transaction that looks like generosity but may be a way to keep others beholden, or to keep yourself small by refusing to receive.

However, examine the actual balance of what moves between you and another. Give only what is truly yours to give, and ask for what you actually need, rather than performing a kindness that quietly debts the other person.
