## IV Emperor

You need a firm frame today, but your attention fragments among a hall of glittering, untested promises.

However, seize one possibility and demand that it survive contact with your own unflinching scrutiny.

Although distraction wears many friendly masks, govern yourself by that single verified boundary rather than a court of ghosts.
