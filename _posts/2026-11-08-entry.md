## IX Nine of Wands

You are standing at the end of a long road, shoulders tight, eyes scanning for what comes next. The vigil itself has become the work; you no longer remember what the first watch was for.

However, lower the guard just enough to let in the recognition that is trying to reach you. Receive the warmth that is being offered rather than insisting the battle is still yours alone to fight.
